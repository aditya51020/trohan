Power Upgrade to 200 Amp

Home / Blog / Power Upgrade to 200 Amp

Many older homes in New Westminster were built with 60 or 100 amp electrical service — enough for the appliances of their era, but often not enough for how homes use power today. A 200 amp service upgrade is one of the most common electrical projects we handle, and for good reason.

Signs You May Need the Upgrade

  • Breakers trip frequently when running multiple appliances at once
  • You're adding an EV charger, hot tub, or central air conditioning
  • Your panel still uses fuses instead of circuit breakers
  • You're renovating or adding a home theatre, sauna, or workshop

What the Process Looks Like

A typical 200 amp upgrade starts with an assessment of your existing panel, service line, and meter, followed by coordination with the utility for a temporary disconnect. Our electricians install the new panel and breakers, upgrade the service line where required, and make sure everything passes inspection before re-energizing the system.

Why It's Worth Doing Properly

A properly sized and installed panel upgrade protects your home from overloaded circuits and reduces the risk of electrical fires, while giving you the headroom to add appliances, EV charging, or smart home devices down the road without redoing the work twice.
