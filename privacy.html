Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

This website has no server-side form handling and no database. When you use the quote request form, your browser opens your own email application with the details you typed already filled in — you decide whether to send that email. The website itself does not store or transmit anything you enter.

We therefore only receive what you choose to email or tell us: your name, email address, phone number, and any project details you include.

2. How We Use Information

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss your project
  • To prepare quotes and arrange site visits
  • We do not sell or rent your information to third parties

3. Data Retention

Enquiries you send us arrive as ordinary email and are kept in our business email account for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet our record-keeping obligations. You can ask us to delete your enquiry at any time using the contact details below.

4. Cookies

This site does not set tracking or advertising cookies and does not use analytics. If that changes, we will update this page.
