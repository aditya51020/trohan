About Our Work

For New Homes, Renovations & Specialized Electrical Work

Whether you are building a new home, renovating an existing property, or need specialized electrical work, Trohan has you covered with a comprehensive range of services.

For new home wiring, we ensure every electrical system is designed and installed to meet the highest standards of safety and efficiency, working closely with builders and homeowners to create customized electrical plans that integrate seamlessly into new construction.

When it comes to electrical remodeling, we excel at upgrading outdated systems and installing new components during renovations — from rewiring and new outlets to updated lighting fixtures and smart home technology — minimizing disruption while maximizing efficiency and safety.
